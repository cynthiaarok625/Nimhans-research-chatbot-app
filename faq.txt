Q: Will my information remain private?
A: Yes. Participant information is stored securely and used only for approved research purposes.

Q: Is participation voluntary?
A: Yes. Participation is completely voluntary and participants may withdraw at any time.

Q: Will I be compensated?
A: Some studies may provide compensation. Details are communicated during the enrollment process.

Q: What types of research studies are currently available?
A: Our hospital conducts a variety of clinical and observational research studies across multiple medical specialties. Available studies may change over time.

Q: How do I know if I am eligible for a study?
A: Each study has specific eligibility requirements. Our research team will review your information and determine whether you meet the criteria for participation.

Q: What happens after I submit my interest form?
A: A member of our research team will review your submission and contact you to discuss potential study opportunities and next steps.

Q: Are there any risks involved in participating in research?
A: Every research study has its own potential risks and benefits. These will be explained in detail before you decide whether to participate.

Q: Can I withdraw from a study after joining?
A: Yes. Participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw from a study at any time without affecting your regular medical care.

Q: Will participating in research affect my current treatment?
A: Participation requirements vary by study. The research team will discuss any potential impact on your current treatment plan before enrollment.

Q: How long do research studies typically last?
A: Study durations vary depending on the research objectives. Some studies may involve a single visit, while others may continue for several months or longer.

Q: Will my personal and medical information be kept confidential?
A: Yes. We follow strict privacy and security protocols to protect participant information and comply with applicable regulations.

Q: Do I need a referral from my doctor to participate?
A: Most studies do not require a referral. However, certain studies may have specific enrollment requirements that will be explained during screening.

Q: Who can I contact if I have additional questions?
A: Our research team is available to answer questions about study participation, eligibility requirements, and the enrollment process.
Q: What is the purpose of the research program?
A: Our research program aims to improve healthcare outcomes through clinical studies and patient-centered research.

Q: Who can participate in research studies?
A: Eligibility varies by study. Participants may be required to meet specific age, health, or demographic criteria.
